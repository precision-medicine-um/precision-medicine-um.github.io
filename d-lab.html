D-Lab · Data & AI

Artificial Intelligence-Based Decision Support Systems

The D-Lab is an AI laboratory applying machine learning and deep learning to large-scale medical data for personalised treatment. Its team includes pioneering inventors of Radiomics and multifactorial Decision Support Systems, with strong experience in translating patient data into ethical, clinically usable tools.

01 — Research line

Radiomics

Developing robust imaging-based biomarkers and predictive models — from handcrafted radiomics to deep learning and foundation models — to characterise disease, predict outcomes, and support clinical decision-making across centres.

  • Handcrafted Radiomics Pipelines: Feature extraction (shape, intensity, texture), stability/reproducibility assessment, and outcome modelling.
  • Deep Learning for Imaging: CNN/Transformer-based detection, segmentation, and prediction directly from images and multimodal inputs.
  • Foundation Models: Leveraging pretrained representations to improve data efficiency and cross-centre robustness.
  • Radiogenomics & Imaging Phenotypes: Linking imaging signatures (radiomics and deep features) to underlying biology and disease heterogeneity.
  • Hybrid Biomarkers (Radiomics + DL + Clinical Data): Integrating engineered features, learned representations, and clinical/omics variables to boost predictive performance while maintaining interpretability and clinical acceptance.
02 — Research line

Trustworthy and Generalisable AI

Ensuring models are reliable across hospitals, scanners, protocols, and populations.

  • Harmonisation: Reducing scanner/protocol batch effects at image and feature level to enable multi-centre generalisation.
  • Explainable & Trustworthy AI: Transparent modelling, calibrated predictions, uncertainty awareness, and clinically meaningful explanations.
  • In Silico AI Validation: Virtual testing and stress-testing to probe robustness before prospective deployment.
  • Synthetic Data: Privacy-aware generation and augmentation to address scarcity, imbalance, and rare subgroups.
03 — Research line

Clinical Decision Support & Patient Decision Aids

Converting complex patient data into actionable, ethically deployed decisions for clinicians and patients.

  • Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS): Integrating imaging, pathology, genomics and clinical variables into validated decision pipelines for diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment selection.
  • Patient Decision Aids (PtDAs): Translating model outputs into patient-facing explanations that support shared decision-making, aligned with clinical pathways and risk communication best practices.
  • Multifactorial Risk Modelling: Combining multiple data sources to estimate outcome probabilities and compare treatment options at the individual level.
  • Clinical Translation: Tooling, usability, and workflow integration so models move from retrospective studies to real-world use.
